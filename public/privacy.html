Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swipess ("Swipess", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Swipess mobile app and website (the "Service"). By using Swipess, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and authentication identifiers (including when you sign in with Apple or Google).
  • Profile & listing content: photos, descriptions, preferences, and any listings, messages, or content you create.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location, used only to power map discovery and nearby matching. You control location permission in your device settings.
  • Usage & device data: in-app interactions, device type, and push-notification tokens used to deliver notifications.
  • Purchase data: records of in-app purchases and subscriptions (processed by Apple; we do not receive your full payment details).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the discovery, matching, and messaging features.
  • To create and secure your account and personalize your experience.
  • To process subscriptions and token purchases.
  • To keep the community safe — including moderating content and enforcing our Terms.
  • To send you notifications you have enabled.

3. Third-Party AI Providers

Swipess offers optional AI features ("Swipess AI", voice-to-text, and automated content moderation). To provide these, we send the relevant input to trusted third-party AI providers:

  • Groq (running Meta Llama models and OpenAI Whisper) — for the AI chat, image moderation, and optional voice transcription.
  • Google Gemini — fallback for AI chat and text/listing assistance.
  • Moonshot AI (Kimi) and MiniMax — additional AI chat fallbacks.

What is sent: only the chat messages you type, the audio you choose to record for transcription, and the images you upload for safety moderation. We do not send your account credentials, contacts, payment data, or private profile data. This data is used only to generate the response or moderation result — it is not sold and is not used to train the providers' models. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in AI chats.

4. Content Moderation

To keep the community safe, images and content may be automatically scanned to detect objectionable material. Content that violates our Terms is removed, and offending accounts may be suspended or banned. See our Terms of Use for our zero-tolerance policy.

5. How We Share Information

  • Identity providers: Apple and Google, only to authenticate your sign-in.
  • Infrastructure & AI providers: as described above, strictly to operate the Service.
  • Payments: Apple (in-app purchases) and, on the web, PayPal.
  • We do not sell your personal data to data brokers.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the in-app Settings. When you do, your personal data is removed from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes the Sign in with Apple grant.

7. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct it, or request deletion at any time. To exercise these rights, use the in-app Settings or contact us below.

8. Children

Swipess is intended only for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit, to protect your data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Contact us at support@swipess.com.
