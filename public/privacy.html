Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swipess ("Swipess", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Swipess mobile app and website (the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email, phone number, authentication identifiers, and account settings.
  • Profile, listings, and communications: photos, descriptions, preferences, listings, reports, support requests, and messages you send through the Service.
  • Verification information: if you choose to request a badge or digital card, documents, images, document details, status records, and information used to review the request or prevent fraud. Government IDs and similar documents may contain sensitive personal information.
  • Location: location you enter for a listing and, if you grant device permission, approximate or precise device location for map and nearby features.
  • Usage, device, and security data: interactions, device and browser details, logs, identifiers, push tokens, crash information, and signals used to prevent abuse.
  • Purchase records: subscription, token, entitlement, and transaction-status information. The payment provider generally processes full payment credentials.
  • AI inputs: text, audio, images, or other content you choose to submit to an AI-enabled feature.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide accounts, listings, discovery, messaging, tokens, subscriptions, and requested services.
  • Review verification requests and display the applicable status or badge.
  • Personalize features, provide support, and send requested or service-related notifications.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, prohibited content, and security incidents; enforce our Terms.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve the Service and comply with legal obligations.

3. What Other Users Can See

Profile and listing information you publish is intended to be visible to other users according to the feature and your available settings. Messages are visible to their participants. A listing may show the location or approximate area you provide. Do not publish an exact home address, identity document, financial information, or other sensitive information unless you intend and need to share it.

A displayed verification status reveals that a particular review or submission was recorded; it does not expose the underlying document to ordinary users and is not a safety guarantee.

4. AI & Moderation Processors

When you use an AI-enabled feature, we may send the content needed for that request to configured AI or moderation processors so they can generate, transcribe, classify, or review it. Automated results can be wrong and may be supplemented by human review. Provider availability and configuration can change as the Service evolves. Do not place account credentials, payment details, identity documents, or confidential legal information into a general-purpose AI chat.

5. How We Share Information

  • Other users: information you publish or intentionally send through marketplace and messaging features.
  • Service providers: hosting, database, authentication, communications, analytics, security, support, AI, and moderation providers that process information to operate the Service.
  • Payment providers and app stores: the provider presented at checkout, to process purchases and manage entitlements.
  • Independent professionals: information you intentionally submit in a legal-service or other professional-service request, so an available provider can assess it.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or address security incidents.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, financing, sale, or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

6. Location & Device Permissions

You can control device permissions in your operating-system settings. Disabling a permission may limit nearby discovery, camera, microphone, photo, or notification features. Removing device permission does not automatically delete location or media you previously chose to publish.

7. Retention & Account Deletion

We keep information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. After a valid deletion request, information is deleted or de-identified according to operational and legal retention schedules. Some records may remain longer in backups, transaction logs, fraud-prevention records, or where law requires or permits retention.

8. Your Choices & Rights

You may update certain profile information and request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent through available settings or by contacting us. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You can manage marketing communications and device permissions separately from essential service messages.

9. Security & International Processing

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption in transit where supported. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, where privacy laws may differ, subject to applicable safeguards.

10. Children

Swipess is intended for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly offer the Service to children. Contact us if you believe a minor has provided personal information.

11. Changes & Contact

We may update this Policy and will post the revised version with a new date. Where required, we will provide additional notice. For privacy questions or requests, email support@swipess.com.
